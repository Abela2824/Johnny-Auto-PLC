# Pitch summary (say this to the client)

1. Your current site is a template that contradicts itself (20 vs 25 years, a 500k-customer claim nobody believes), runs a blog borrowed from a US parts shop, and gives a customer nothing to do except press one of five WhatsApp buttons.
2. The new site tells the story you actually have: a 100-person engineering company with an Italian paint booth and a fibreglass department that is heading to EV assembly in Gelan. One line runs from the Megenagna bays to the Gelan assembly line, in English and Amharic.
3. The four features that matter most: online booking with real slots, a photo quote where customers mark the damage on a car diagram, a live tracker that shows which of your six stations their car is at, and the staff board that drives both. Together they end the "where is my car?" phone calls and pre-qualify every collision job before it arrives.
4. Two more that make money: a fleet portal for NGOs, embassies and companies (one account, monthly statement, approvals from their desk), and an EV plant interest list that turns the Future page into evidence for investors and early fleet buyers.
5. Everything you clicked today runs on demo data behind a documented API; the backend scope (phased MVP → fleet and payments → insurance and warranty) is written, and the front end is ready to connect the day the first endpoint exists.
