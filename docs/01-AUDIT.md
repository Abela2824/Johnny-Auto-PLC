# Audit — johnnyautoplc.com (checked 20 Oct 2026)

Pages inspected: `/` (index.php), `about.php`, `works.php` ("Why Us"), `gallery.php`, `contact.php`.

## The business, as the site states it
Johnny General Auto Car Maintenance & Assembly P.L.C (JGACMA). Privately owned. Megenagna, Addis Ababa, near the Ethiopian Standards Authority. 2,000 m² plot, 100+ staff. Collision and body repair, Nova Verta downdraft paint booth with "Total Control Technology", electronic diagnostics, a fibreglass department that also repairs boats, "one-stop-shop" (towing, mechanical, key programming, alarms). Vision: lead in collision repair technology. Mission: "best automotive repair service in East Africa", care for high-end vehicles. Future: an electric-car assembly plant in Gelan (Oromia Special Zone surrounding Finfinne), 5,000 m², 780–1,000 SKD units/year.

## Findings (15)

1. **Identity is split four ways.** "Johnny General Auto Car Maintenance & Assembly P.L.C", "JGACMA", "Johnny Auto" (on the trucks, vans and roof sign) and "Johnny Auto General" (newsletter). Two email domains (johnnyautoplc.com and a Yahoo address). The strongest asset, the "Johnny Auto" mark everyone in Megenagna recognises, is the least used.
2. **Years in business contradict each other on the same page.** Heading, stat strip and footer say "25 years"; the About body and Why-Us copy say "the past 20 years" / "over the past twenty years". Needs one number and a founding year.
3. **"+500k happy customers" hurts credibility.** It implies roughly 55 cars a day, every day, for 25 years. Unverifiable and implausible for one 2,000 m² shop. Replace with countable claims (vehicles per month, fleet contracts, booth cycles).
4. **The EV plant story is unresolved.** "Our Goal" says an "auto assembly plant" is "under evaluation"; the Future Project block says an "electric cars assembly plant" with a site, size and capacity. No timeline, no status, no call to action. This is the most interesting thing about the company and it reads like an afterthought.
5. **The blog is borrowed.** All three posts are excerpts from theautopartsshop.com (a US parts retailer), credited to "Admin"; titles link to "#" and "read more" sends visitors to the other company's store.
6. **WhatsApp is everywhere and nowhere.** Five identical WhatsApp buttons on the home page (all wa.me/251944294429), zero on the Contact page. The +251 94 429 4429 number appears only on home/contact; +251 911 717197 appears on about/gallery. Four numbers in total across pages, presented as three.
7. **Generic template, vendor-branded.** Bootstrap/jQuery theme with hero slider slogans, three- and four-card rows, "Learn More" buttons that go to "#", keyword filler ("Auto Body Shop in Addis") and a "Design by Alpha IT Solution" logo linked on every page. The vendor gets more brand placement than the client's Amharic name, which appears only inside a team photo.
8. **Load weight.** Home page: 15 scripts (jQuery, jQuery UI, Bootstrap, Owl Carousel, Stellar, Countdown, Datepicker, Fancybox, YTPlayer, AOS...), 13 stylesheets (one loaded twice), 33 `<img>` tags, about 2.0 MB of assets, no lazy-loading, no responsive images. Most visitors in Addis are on phones.
9. **Duplicates and dead links.** logo.png used twice plus logo2.png plus johnny-logo-large.png on one page; PPG logo appears twice in the partner strip; Trane logo links to pallmax.se (a Swedish reseller); every service card links to `index.php#services`; empty `[](#)` anchors on every page; page title of Contact is "Services".
10. **Typos and copy quality.** "Cmpetitors", "engine over hall", "sol utions", "qual ity", "Well- trained", "Fiber glass"/"Fiberglass", "JGACMA continue", "Near to Ethiopian Standards Authority", "Car Shuts Off While Driving & It's Fix?", and a missing verb in "to set itself apart ... and leadership in the market".
11. **No conversion path.** "Get A Quote" / "Request A Quote" buttons have no form behind them on the home page. The contact form has First/Last/Email/Tel/Message and no visible submit action. No booking, no vehicle fields, no photo upload, no working hours, no map, no prices or turnaround expectations.
12. **Gallery tells no story.** 36 images in one flat grid, every alt text is "Image", no captions, no categories, no before/after. The photos are actually good evidence (Nissan Patrol in primer then in colour, a stretched limousine in prep, a tanker trailer, the branded tow truck and van, a 50-person team photo from May 2020) but nothing explains them.
13. **Partner logos without a relationship.** PPG, Nova Verta, Yamaha, Honda Marine and Trane are shown as "Quality Products We Use". Yamaha and Honda Marine make sense next to boat fibreglass work, but the site never connects them; Trane (climate) is unexplained.
14. **The best differentiators are buried in supplier copy.** The Nova Verta section is pasted booth-manufacturer text (VFD, self-diagnostics, pressure control) rather than what it means for a customer: dust-free, baked finish, colour-matched. Boat repair, towing, fleet intake and the team's size are mentioned in passing.
15. **Zero trust signals.** No founding year, no named leadership, no certifications, no insurer or fleet-client relationships, no warranty statement, no testimonials, no Amharic. For a company courting corporate fleets and an EV plant partner, the site offers nothing a procurement officer or investor could cite.

## What is genuinely strong (and should lead)
- A real Italian Nova Verta downdraft booth, visible in several photos.
- A fibreglass department that also does boats: unique positioning in Addis.
- 100+ uniformed staff, a branded tow truck and service van: a company, not a garage.
- An EV assembly ambition with a named site and capacity.
- The "Johnny Auto" mark and the red/blue brand colours are already known on the street.
