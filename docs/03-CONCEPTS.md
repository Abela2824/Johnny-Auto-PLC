# Creative direction

The story to tell: a 25-year Addis Ababa institution that is not just a garage but an automotive engineering company heading toward EV assembly. Heritage plus future.

## Concept A — SIGNAL
**One line:** The shop as a diagnostics instrument: every element is a readout.
**Mood:** Night shift. Precise, cold, trustworthy. The glow of a scanner screen on an engine bay.
**Palette:** Carbon `#0B0D10`, phosphor green `#9BFF6E`, instrument amber `#FFB020`, white.
**Type:** Space Grotesk (display) + JetBrains Mono (readouts).
**Signature interaction:** A horizontal scan line follows scroll. Whatever it passes gets "read": photos switch from duotone to full colour, numbers resolve from noise, the hero car wireframe reveals layer by layer (body → paint → electronics).
**Risk:** Reads as a tech start-up. Under-sells the heat and craft of a body shop. Green is not in the brand.

## Concept B — WET PAINT
**One line:** Liquid colour as the brand: the whole site is one paint job.
**Mood:** Cinematic and sensual. Nova Verta white light, PPG colour, gloss.
**Palette:** Primer grey `#C9C6BF`, booth white `#F7F7F5`, deep black `#0E0E0E`, one "fresh coat" red `#E2342D` that spreads across the page.
**Type:** Unbounded (wide, bold display) + Archivo (text).
**Signature interaction:** A scroll-driven paint pass. A spray-gun gradient sweeps across the hero car and headline leaving gloss behind; gallery images carry a wet sheen that moves with the pointer; the booth section is a light-box with animated infrared lamps.
**Risk:** Beautiful but narrow. Paint is one of six services and says nothing about EV assembly or fleets.

## Concept C — THE LINE
**One line:** From the service line to the assembly line.
**Idea:** A single continuous line runs through the site. It begins as the bay floor markings in Megenagna, becomes the repair pipeline (intake → diagnosis → body → paint → QC → handover), and ends as the assembly line in Gelan. The accent colour travels with it: brand red for heat, metal and twenty-five years of work; brand blue, cooled to electric, for the EV future. The page itself moves from heritage to future.
**Mood:** Industrial, confident, warm-to-cool. Ethiopian, not generic: Amharic sits beside English as a first-class language, not a footnote.
**Palette:** Ink `#0A0C10`, Bone `#F2EEE6`, Signal Red `#E2342D`, Volt Blue `#2F6BFF` → `#5CE1FF`, Steel `#2A303A`.
**Type:** Archivo (variable width: condensed headlines and expanded labels from one family, so it reads like workshop signage) + JetBrains Mono (ticket numbers, readouts) + Noto Sans Ethiopic (Amharic).
**Signature interaction:** The hero car draws itself as a wireframe, then a sweep of colour paints it front to back. The same line continues as a scroll-progress rail down the page. In the Future section the rail turns blue and the car silhouette gives way to an EV skateboard platform whose battery cells light in sequence.

## The pick: THE LINE

It is the only concept that tells the whole story the client needs told, heritage and future in one visual device, instead of decorating a single service. It elevates the identity they already own on the street (the logo's red and royal blue) by giving each colour a meaning, so the client sees themselves rather than a template. And the line is also product UI: the same pipeline graphic in the hero is the repair tracker for customers and the board for staff, which makes the backend need visible from the first screen.
